**Sanny Builder Error Messages**
          
      
        
        
          
          

            

            *Couldn't complete jump to label. Internal label not found*

            

This error message is displayed  as one of the high-level statements ([if..then..else..end](conditions.htm#THEN), [while..end](loops.htm#WHILE..END), etc) has incorrect syntax. In most cases, it happens when you did not  write a closing end at the end of a statement.

            

**Possible solutions:** consult with the help and check out the correctness of every statement.
