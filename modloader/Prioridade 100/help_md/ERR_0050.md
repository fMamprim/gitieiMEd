*Not enough actual parameters*

            

This error occurs as the  compiler failed to get needed number of parameters either for an [additional command](additional.htm) or [directive](directives.htm). Either there are parameters less than needed, or some of them are unacceptable.  You may know needed number of parameters at the proper help topic, and in the  hint shown after [Ctrl+Shift+Space](hotkeys.htm#space) are pressed.

            

**Possible solutions:** write  the required number of parameters.
