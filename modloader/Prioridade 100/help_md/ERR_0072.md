**Sanny Builder Error Messages**
          
      
        
        
          
          

            

            *Incorrect number of the conditions*

            

This error occurs in the [IF..END](conditions.htm#IF_AND) statement, when you used several conditions at once  without specifying a mode of checking: **AND** or **OR**.

           

 **Possible solutions:** correct this statement: either use the only  condition, or write the word **AND** or **OR**.
