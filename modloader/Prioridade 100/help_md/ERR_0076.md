            *ondition expected*

            

Its a common error  displayed if you did not specify a condition to check after words [while](loops.htm#WHILE..END) or [until](loops.htm#REPEAT..UNTIL). These constructs require at least one condition. You may use pre-defined constants **True** or **False** as a fake condition. 

            

**Possible solutions:** write a condition to check
