**Sanny Builder Error Messages**
          
      
        
        
          
          

            

            *Use positive integer value*

            

Its a common error  occurred when you used a negative number or not a number at all (for example, a  string) where the compiler expected to find a positive integer number ([indexed variables](variables.htm#Index), [Alloc](additional.htm#ALLOC), etc).

            

**Possible solutions:** use acceptable positive integer number
